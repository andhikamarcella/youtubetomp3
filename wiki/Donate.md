# Donate — just pay what you can

Donations are optional. They do not unlock features or change support priority.

- Ko-fi (global): <https://ko-fi.com/cellauu>
- Saweria (Indonesia): <https://saweria.co/dhikamarcella>

Run `ytconv donate`, or press `N` on the interactive home screen. After a successful browser handoff, YTConv returns home after five seconds. If the device has no compatible browser command, YTConv copies or prints the selected link.
